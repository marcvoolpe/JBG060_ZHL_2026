Which cropland is under water?

A flood trigger multiplies two maps: where the water is, and where the fields are. Seven free cropland maps disagree about the fields. We labelled 450 boxes of 210 m, each twice and blind, checked every map against them, and built a map of our own. This page shows what the checked numbers change for flood exposure and for anticipatory action.

Our map in two questions

Rule 1, a decision tree of depth 2 fitted on the 125 calibration points, frozen before any test label was read:

  1. Is there a building within 333 m of the box (Google Open Buildings)?
  2. Does the box show field edges in the green season (edge density of Sentinel-2)?

Both yes: the box holds a field. A person can check it on an image. It finds more fields than any public map, and it also marks some boxes next to homesteads that have no field.

What it changes for ZOA

Which map to use where

  • Aweil: WorldCereal 2021 or our map. Avoid Esri and Dynamic World: they miss almost every field (recall 0.03 and 0.02).
  • Bor South: avoid Dynamic World, which calls open grassland crop (44 of 74 empty boxes). Only the labelled sample gives a reliable number there, and it is wide.
  • Anywhere: never choose a map by its overall accuracy. Where crop is rare, a map that finds nothing still scores 90% or more.

6 · For ZOA and the next group

What we hand over

Every step behind these numbers can be run again: label more boxes, fit a better model, map a new area or season, and rebuild this page. All of it is in the repository, folder cropland/, with the protocol in METHODOLOGY.md.

How a next group builds on this

    What stays fixed, because it makes the numbers trustworthy: two blind labellers per box, nobody settling their disagreements, the presence rule on the 210 m box, models fitted on calibration points only and frozen before the test labels are read, and stratified estimators with an interval for every number.

    7 · How sure are we

    How sure, and what the numbers cannot say

    How sure

      Limits

      • One season (2025) and two areas. Cropland in earlier flood years is today's, so those years are a scenario.
      • The reference is what two people see on satellite images, not a field visit.
      • Bor South holds almost no cropland in the sample (120 boxes), so its numbers are wide and no recall can be computed there.
      • Random boxes hardly touch flooded land: in 2025 only 1 Aweil and 2 Bor South boxes lay in a flood cell, so the sample cannot judge the 2025 flood. A flood-prone stratum fixes this next season.
      • County and national numbers from maps are map estimates, not checked. Our national map is not checked outside Aweil and Bor South: there are no labels there.
      • Exposure is an upper bound on damage: flood timing, duration and crop stage decide the loss. No forecast is used here.
